What they do

Incorporated in March 2005, SFC Environmental Technologies Limited is an environmental technology company engaged in providing technology, design, engineering, manufacturing, supply, installation and commissioning solutions for wastewater treatment (“WWT”), wastewater recycling and reuse (“WRR”), and solid waste treatment (“SWT”). The Company’s wastewater treatment business is primarily based on its proprietary C-Tech technology, an advanced sequencing batch reactor (“SBR”) process used in sewage treatment plants and industrial effluent treatment. Its solutions include technology packages, equipment, automation systems, installation, commissioning and related services.

More about the company

The Company’s solid waste treatment business is based on its proprietary Organic Extraction (“OREX”) technology, designed to separate biodegradable organic matter from mixed municipal waste to support biogas generation and resource recovery. It provides end-to-end solutions covering design, engineering, construction, equipment supply, commissioning and operations and maintenance. The Company operates two integrated solid waste treatment facilities in Goa with an aggregate treatment capacity of up to 425 tonnes per day. Through its subsidiary, it has also secured orders for compressed biogas plants in Assam and Odisha, with cumulative order value of ₹5,017 million, and has expanded into projects involving agricultural waste and biomass.

The Company operates across 23 states and five Union Territories in India, with a business model involving technology supply, engineering solutions, project execution and operations and maintenance. Its customer relationships include engineering, procurement and construction companies, municipal bodies and government-owned entities. The Company’s growth strategy includes expanding its wastewater recycling and reuse capabilities and pursuing opportunities in solid waste treatment, compressed biogas and waste-to-energy projects.

As of August 31, 2026, the Company had 264 employees, including 149 engineers, supported by teams across design and engineering, electrical and instrumentation, erection and commissioning, manufacturing, proposals, sales, finance and other functions.

  • Diversified Growth Opportunities: Presence across wastewater treatment, wastewater recycling and reuse, solid waste treatment, compressed biogas and waste-to-energy projects.

What they do

Incorporated in September 2022, Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited is a precision engineering company engaged in the manufacturing, trading and servicing of critical components for underwater pelletizing systems. The Company manufactures a range of components for underwater pelletizing systems, including pelletizing die plates, pelletizing blades and knives, blade holders, cutter hubs, tapped driving bushes and other spare parts. Its products cater to polymer compounding, masterbatches, engineering plastics, cable compounds, specialty polymers and plastics recycling industries.

More about the company

The Company commenced operations at its company-owned manufacturing facility at Waghodia GIDC, Vadodara, Gujarat, in July 2025 and has progressively transitioned manufacturing activities from its earlier leased facility in Makarpura, Vadodara. Its manufacturing infrastructure includes precision machining and testing equipment such as VMC, CNC, milling, EDM, wire-cut, lathe and surface-grinding machines. Certain specialised processes, including heat treatment, hardening and coating, are outsourced to job workers. The Company also provides refurbishment and repair services for used die plates, intermediary support for the marketing and supply of CJTEK components, and management consulting services for developing customer relationships.

The Company serves customers across India and international markets, including the USA, Germany, the Netherlands, Israel, Saudi Arabia, Japan, Australia, Italy and other countries. It holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for manufacturing and supplying pelletizing system components and received ZED Bronze Certification under the MSME Sustainable Certification Scheme in November 2025. Its operations are supported by an experienced promoter and management team, technical personnel and dedicated sales and marketing capabilities.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 71 full-time employees, excluding Directors, across production and operations, quality and testing, design, sales and marketing, finance, administration and other functions.

What they do

Incorporated in November 2020, Johnsoncab Electricals Limited is engaged in the manufacturing of low voltage wires and cables up to 1.1 kV, catering to power infrastructure, transmission and distribution, renewable energy, railways and allied industrial applications. The company manufactures and sells its products under the brand name “JOHNSON CABLES” and caters to both private sector customers and government entities.

More about the company

The company has a diversified product portfolio comprising control cables, power cables, industrial single and multicore control cables, housing and building wires, welding cables, solar/photovoltaic cables and submersible flat cables. It operates two manufacturing facilities in Mehsana, Gujarat, with a combined installed capacity of 79,950 KMS per annum as of Fiscal 2026. The manufacturing facilities are equipped with machinery for wire drawing, annealing, bunching, insulation, extrusion, testing and packaging.

Johnsoncab Electricals serves customers through direct orders, tender participation and vendor approval processes, with its products supported by ISO and BIS certifications. The company continues to invest in manufacturing infrastructure and product innovation to support its growth.

As of March 31, 2026, the company had 167 employees across various functions including production, operations, quality assurance, sales and marketing, supply chain, finance and administration.
