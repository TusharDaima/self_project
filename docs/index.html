What they do

Incorporated in June 2014, Core4 Engineers Limited is an Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Commissioning (“EPCC”) company providing integrated infrastructure solutions across railway infrastructure, power transmission and distribution, and water and wastewater treatment. More about the company

Its railway infrastructure activities include overhead electrification, traction substations, station redevelopment, civil works and allied signalling and telecommunication interfaces. In power transmission and distribution, it undertakes turnkey projects involving substations, transmission lines and distribution networks up to 220 kV. The Company also undertakes subcontracting assignments, providing on-ground execution support for engineering and infrastructure projects.

The Company serves government organizations, public sector undertakings, utilities, infrastructure contractors and private enterprises across India. Its business model combines multidisciplinary project execution, in-house design and engineering capabilities, panel manufacturing and vendor partnerships to deliver integrated infrastructure solutions.

What they do

Incorporated in March 2005, SFC Environmental Technologies Limited is an environmental technology company engaged in providing technology, design, engineering, manufacturing, supply, installation and commissioning solutions for wastewater treatment (“WWT”), wastewater recycling and reuse (“WRR”), and solid waste treatment (“SWT”). The Company’s wastewater treatment business is primarily based on its proprietary C-Tech technology, an advanced sequencing batch reactor (“SBR”) process used in sewage treatment plants and industrial effluent treatment. Its solutions include technology packages, equipment, automation systems, installation, commissioning and related services.

More about the company

The Company’s solid waste treatment business is based on its proprietary Organic Extraction (“OREX”) technology, designed to separate biodegradable organic matter from mixed municipal waste to support biogas generation and resource recovery. It provides end-to-end solutions covering design, engineering, construction, equipment supply, commissioning and operations and maintenance. The Company operates two integrated solid waste treatment facilities in Goa with an aggregate treatment capacity of up to 425 tonnes per day. Through its subsidiary, it has also secured orders for compressed biogas plants in Assam and Odisha, with cumulative order value of ₹5,017 million, and has expanded into projects involving agricultural waste and biomass.

The Company operates across 23 states and five Union Territories in India, with a business model involving technology supply, engineering solutions, project execution and operations and maintenance. Its customer relationships include engineering, procurement and construction companies, municipal bodies and government-owned entities. The Company’s growth strategy includes expanding its wastewater recycling and reuse capabilities and pursuing opportunities in solid waste treatment, compressed biogas and waste-to-energy projects.

As of August 31, 2026, the Company had 264 employees, including 149 engineers, supported by teams across design and engineering, electrical and instrumentation, erection and commissioning, manufacturing, proposals, sales, finance and other functions.

What they do

Incorporated in August 2024, Antala Industries Limited is engaged in the manufacturing, processing, printing, laminating and supply of industrial and commercial packaging products. The Company specializes in BOPP bags, BOPP printing rolls, BOPP lamination rolls, PP woven bags and PP woven lamination rolls, serving customers across industries such as food processing, fertilizers, chemicals, cement and seeds. The Company operates an integrated manufacturing facility in Rajkot, Gujarat, with capabilities including extrusion, coating and lamination, rotogravure printing, stitching and finishing.
