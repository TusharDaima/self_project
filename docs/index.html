What they do

Incorporated in June 2022, Shree Siddhivinayak Greentech Industries Limited is an integrated agro-processing company engaged in the manufacturing of Jaggery Powder, Khandsari Sugar, jaggery-based value-added food products, and sugarcane by-products. The company also operates a Cane Seed nursery, enabling backward integration in sugarcane cultivation. It serves both B2B customers through jaggery powder, Khandsari sugar, and by-products, and B2C customers through its "Feel Guddy" branded jaggery-based premix products, including Jaggery Tea, Jaggery Coffee, Instant Rice Kheer, Jaggery Laddoo, and Jaggery Lapsi.

More about the company

The company follows an integrated sugarcane processing model, utilizing sugarcane to manufacture primary products while efficiently monetizing by-products such as bagasse, molasses, press mud, and ash. With backward integration into sugarcane seed cultivation, captive processing facilities, and a focus on sustainable and zero-waste manufacturing, the company has established a diversified product portfolio catering to both industrial and retail markets.

Shree Siddhivinayak Greentech Industries emphasizes operational efficiency, quality control, sustainable manufacturing practices, and product innovation.

As of March 31, 2026, the company employed 155 permanent personnel across production, engineering, agriculture, administration, sales, finance, security, and other operational functions.

What they do

Incorporated on February 17, 2014, Airpro Technology India Limited is a technology-driven company engaged in the design, assembly, distribution and servicing of wired and wireless networking products and solutions. The Company operates as a brand owner, providing networking solutions for enterprises and institutions to establish and manage reliable, scalable and cost-effective connectivity infrastructure. The Company offers a range of networking products, including wireless access points, WLAN controllers, Ethernet switches, gateways, outdoor wireless solutions, 4G/5G Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) and industrial Power over Ethernet (PoE) switches.

More about the company

In addition to hardware products, the Company provides software-enabled networking solutions through its cloud-based platform, AirproCloud, which enables customers to configure, monitor and manage network infrastructure remotely. The platform supports centralised monitoring of networking devices, bandwidth utilisation, user access controls and network performance analytics and is currently available on Android-based devices. The Company also develops customised firmware for certain white-label products, supporting customer-specific requirements such as user authentication, bandwidth allocation and network access management.

The Company follows a hybrid business model comprising product design, firmware customisation, cloud-based network management, in-house assembly and white-label networking solutions. It markets its products under its own brand through distributors and online channel partners, including e-commerce platforms, and primarily operates through a B2B model. Its customer base spans multiple industry verticals.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 39 permanent employees across management, sales and marketing, software development, legal and secretarial, accounts and finance, IT and technical support, and support functions.

Its O&M services include production support, plant operations, preventive and breakdown maintenance, equipment monitoring and technical support.

More about the company

The Company operates a dedicated metal fabrication facility at Kapasan, Chittorgarh, Rajasthan, with an annual installed capacity of 4,000 MT. Its execution capabilities are supported by an in-house fleet of 232 industrial vehicles and equipment, including forklifts, trucks, excavators, cranes, hydra machines, bulldozers and loaders. This equipment base, together with its technical workforce, enables the Company to manage site-level logistics, mobilise resources and execute operational and project-related activities across multiple locations.

It operates across seven Indian states and has an overseas subsidiary, Monomark Engineering FZE, in the United Arab Emirates, through which it provides O&M services to Fujairah Gold FZC.
