What they do

Incorporated in April 1997, HD Fire Protect Limited is an Indian manufacturer and supplier of fire protection equipment and systems, offering water, foam and gas-based fire suppression solutions. The Company offers a comprehensive portfolio across eight product categories, including sprinklers, alarm valves and accessories, deluge valves and systems, foam equipment and suppression systems, monitors and nozzles, water spray nozzles, custom-engineered systems and gas suppression systems. Its products cater to industrial, residential and commercial applications, including oil and gas, refining and petrochemicals, power and energy, heavy engineering, aerospace, pharmaceuticals, data centres, hospitality, healthcare and other sectors.

More about the company

The Company has over three decades of experience and has established a global presence, with exports to more than 90 countries since inception and 49 countries during the three months ended June 30, 2026. It was the first Indian fire protection equipment manufacturer to receive UL Listing for deluge valves in 1996 and the first Indian manufacturer to receive FM approval for a range of low-expansion foam system products. As of September 2026, it had 21 UL Listed and 87 FM Approved product certifications. The Company operates two manufacturing facilities in Jalgaon and Thane, Maharashtra, covering approximately 8.50 acres, supported by a fire test laboratory and R&D capabilities.

The Company has supplied fire protection equipment and systems to 2,066 customers in Fiscal 2026 and has supplied products for major projects including the Samruddhi Mahamarg, Jamnagar refinery, new Indian Parliament building, Saudi Aramco's Ras Tanura Sea Island site and Oman's Integrated Power & Water Plant Project. Revenue from operations increased from ₹3,729.53 million in Fiscal 2024 to ₹4,892.81 million in Fiscal 2026, representing a CAGR of 14.54%, while its order book stood at ₹1,586.01 million as of June 30, 2026.

As of June 30, 2026, the Company had 279 permanent employees and 253 contractual employees.

  • Strong product certification and quality track record.

  • Track record of profitability and consistent financial performance.

What they do

Incorporated in December 2007, Ravita Engineering Services Limited is an engineering solutions company engaged in Engineering, Procurement, Installation and Commissioning (EPIC) of HVAC systems, central air-conditioning solutions, air flow systems, chiller plants, industrial compressors, cooling equipment and other electromechanical equipment on a turnkey basis. The Company also provides Operations and Maintenance (O&M) services for projects executed by it and systems installed by third-party solution providers.The Company operates across three areas: Onshore, Offshore and Data Center. Its Onshore operations cover commercial, hospitality, corporate and industrial establishments, while its Offshore operations include rigs, platforms and other offshore marine installations.

More about the company

During the last three fiscal years and the three months ended June 30, 2026, the Company undertook operations at 6 rigs and 16 associated offshore oil and gas platforms. Its Data Center business provides O&M services for cooling systems, chiller plants, HVAC equipment and mechanical utilities, and it has also secured data center infrastructure work orders aggregating ₹12,000.00 lakhs in Maharashtra.

As of June 30, 2026, the Company had an order book across its Onshore, Offshore and Data Center operations, comprising EPIC, O&M and Hybrid projects. The Company is led by Vibhoar Agrawal, Chairman and Executive Director, and Sunildutt Narayan Goswami, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, supported by its Chief Operating Officer and engineering and execution teams.

The Company's revenue from operations increased from ₹1,349.25 lakhs in Fiscal 2024 to ₹27,763.29 lakhs in Fiscal 2026, while EBITDA increased to ₹4,004.92 lakhs and profit after tax stood at ₹2,804.16 lakhs in Fiscal 2026.

As of June 30, 2026, the Company had 399 permanent employees, including 369 in site operations.

What they do

Incorporated in February 2009, Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited is a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) providing unsecured personal loans to salaried and self-employed individuals through its fully digital lending platform. The Company provides personal loans under the “LoanFront” brand through its mobile application, which supports loan origination, customer verification, due diligence, underwriting, documentation, disbursal and repayment. It provides loans through both direct lending and co-lending arrangements with other NBFCs and also generates revenue by providing application and technology services to other NBFCs.

More about the company

The Company follows a technology-enabled lending model supported by digital verification, credit assessment, credit decisioning and risk evaluation tools. It has been operating its digital lending business through the LoanFront platform for the last six years. The Company was acquired by CapFront Technologies Private Limited in October 2019, following which it continued to expand its digital lending operations. As of July 31, 2026, the Company had Assets Under Management (AUM) of ₹812.41 crore, net worth of ₹471.96 crore and a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.80.

As of July 31, 2026, the Company had 56 employees, including 17 employees in technology, 11 in operations and 11 in risk functions. Its operations are supported by an experienced management team with expertise in digital lending and financial services.

What they do

Incorporated in 2019, Jio Platforms Limited is a technology-driven digital services company that operates through its subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited. The company provides digital connectivity and technology solutions across India, serving over 524 million customers as of March 31, 2026.

More about the company

The company has developed a proprietary technology stack with capabilities spanning network infrastructure, software platforms, operating systems, devices, and digital applications. Its services cater to both consumers and enterprises through a combination of digital platforms and a nationwide distribution network.

For consumers, Jio Platforms offers mobile and fixed broadband connectivity, digital entertainment, gaming, cloud storage, smart home solutions, and AI-enabled digital services. Its ecosystem includes platforms such as MyJio, JioTV+, JioSaavn, JioGames, and JioAICloud.

For businesses, the company provides enterprise connectivity, cloud solutions, IoT services, unified communications platforms, managed services, security solutions, and private 5G offerings. Its enterprise customer base includes organizations across banking, utilities, transportation, manufacturing, government, and information technology sectors.

As of March 31, 2026, Jio Platforms and its subsidiaries had 28,163 full-time employees and a portfolio of patent applications covering digital connectivity, cloud-native networks, artificial intelligence, and related technologies.
